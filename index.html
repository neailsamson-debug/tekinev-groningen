Welcome to Tekinev

Warm light, open fire, a seat at the table.

Tekinev is a Turkish restaurant in the Groningen city centre, serving grilled meats, oven-baked börek, meze and sweets for dinner with friends, family or a quiet evening out.

Guests describe it as a cosy place with attentive service, generous portions and an atmosphere that feels like home — with plenty of vegan options on the menu.
